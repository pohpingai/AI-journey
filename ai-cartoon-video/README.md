## 🌟 About This Project

The idea was to create a short and simple educational video:

1. A cute girl smiles.
2. Her pet dog appears.
3. The girl points at the dog and says **“Dog.”**

The completed video combines AI-generated pictures, animation, voice-over and background music.

## 💡 What I Learned

This was one of the harder projects in my AI journey because the pictures generated by AI do not always match what I have in mind.

I learned that **creating the right character is the backbone of the whole project**. When the character is not right from the beginning, it becomes difficult to maintain consistency across the different scenes.

Creating a picture is only the first step. Adding natural movement, facial expressions and personality to the character requires another set of skills.

I hope to learn how to add more movements to my characters and make my future animations feel more natural and lively.

## 🛠️ Tool Used

I used **OpenArt** for this creative journey.

## 🎨 Creative Process

**Idea → Character creation → Scene generation → Animation → Voice-over → Music → Final editing**

This project taught me that even a simple 15-second video can require many experiments, adjustments and creative decisions.

## 🚀 What I Hope to Improve

- Add more character movements
- Create more natural facial expressions
- Improve character consistency between scenes
- Make the animation smoother and more lifelike
